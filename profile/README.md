# Claude Cowork Training

Free, tested resources for teams learning **Claude Cowork** — Anthropic's agentic desktop app that works directly with your files and folders. Everything here is built around one rule: it has to have been run in Cowork at least once before we publish it. Full guides, screenshots, and live team training live at **[claudecoworktraining.com](https://claudecoworktraining.com)**.

## What we publish

📗 **[cowork-course](https://github.com/claude-cowork-training/cowork-course)** — a free 10-lesson course taught *interactively inside Claude itself*. No videos: download the folder, point Cowork at it, and say "start lesson 1.1." Claude becomes your instructor, sets exercises on the included practice files, and tracks your progress. Web version: the [free Claude Cowork course](https://claudecoworktraining.com/course/).

⚙️ **[claude-cowork-team-workflows](https://github.com/claude-cowork-training/claude-cowork-team-workflows)** — 25 copy-paste team workflows across reporting, meetings, marketing, finance, and onboarding, each with the exact prompt, one-time setup, and a labeled time-saved estimate. Mirrors the [25 team workflows guide](https://claudecoworktraining.com/guides/25-team-workflows/).

✍️ **[claude-cowork-prompts](https://github.com/claude-cowork-training/claude-cowork-prompts)** — 70 tested, Cowork-native prompts organized by task, every one referencing real files, folders, or outputs.

All examples use one consistent fictional company (Metamorfeus) — synthetic data only, never real names or clients. Time-saved figures are estimates, labeled with their basis. Everything is **CC BY 4.0**: use it at work, share it, adapt it — just credit claudecoworktraining.com.

Rolling Cowork out across a team? We run [live team training workshops](https://claudecoworktraining.com/teams/training/).

Created and maintained by [Peter Velinov](https://claudecoworktraining.com/about/), Claude Certified Architect – Foundations and founder of [Metamorfeus](https://metamorfeus.com). Metamorfeus, the example company in these materials, is a fictionalized version of his real consultancy — all course data is synthetic.
